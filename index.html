CIRCLE SOLUTIONS INC.
RETAIL MARKET MANAGER SALES CONTRACT

Independent Contractor Agreement — Performance-Based Compensation

This Agreement is entered into on {{today_date}} between Circle Solutions Inc. ("Company") and {{agent_name}} ("Manager").

1. Independent Contractor Status

Manager is engaged as an independent contractor, not an employee, of the Company. Manager is responsible for their own tax obligations, is not entitled to employee benefits, and controls the manner and means of managing assigned store(s) within the scope of this Agreement and Company policy. This Agreement does not create a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship.

2. Compensation Structure

Manager compensation has two components: (1) hourly-vs-personal-commission pay (either/or), and (2) a flat override commission on supervised/trained agents. There is no flat salary and no weekly spiff or bonus of any kind.

2.1 Personal Pay — Either/Or Rule (Not Both): Each week, Manager is paid whichever amount is GREATER — never both:

  • Hourly Pay: $10.00 per logged working hour
  • Personal Commission: $200.00 per personal Qualified, Activated Enrollment

2.2 Override Commission (Per Supervised/Trained Agent): In addition to Manager's own either/or pay above, Manager earns a flat $25.00 override on every Qualified, Activated Enrollment closed by each agent Manager trains or oversees. This override is a flat rate — it does not change based on the agent's volume — and stacks on top of Manager's personal pay.

Example: Manager personally closes 3 enrollments (earning $600, since that beats $400 hourly) and supervises one agent who closes 6 enrollments that week. Manager earns an additional $25 × 6 = $150 in override, for a total of $750 that week.

A "Qualified, Activated Enrollment" means an enrollment that has been submitted, approved by the carrier/utility, and installed/activated on the customer's account. All commission (personal and override) is contingent on each Qualified, Activated Enrollment remaining active with the carrier/utility for a minimum of thirty (30) days. If an enrollment is canceled, reversed, or charged back within that window, all associated commission — including any override commission earned on that enrollment — is subject to reversal and may be deducted from future pay.

3. Performance Requirements

  • Manager Minimum: Manager must personally close five (5) or more enrollments per week. This is a hard floor.
  • Supervised Agent Minimum: Each agent under Manager's supervision must average no less than three (3) enrollments per week (subject to that agent's own individual 2-week new-hire grace period).
  • Store Goal: Each assigned store should reach ten (10) or more combined enrollments per week as the overall performance target.

IMPORTANT: Failure to meet the personal minimum of five (5) enrollments per week is grounds for suspension or termination review. This is a performance-based, commission-driven role. Manager's primary responsibility is to keep assigned stores staffed, trained, and performing — compensation is structured to reward results, not presence.

4. At-Will Relationship (Texas)

This Agreement and the underlying contractor relationship are at-will. Either Party may terminate this Agreement, or the underlying working relationship, at any time, with or without cause and with or without advance notice, consistent with Texas law governing independent contractor and at-will relationships.

5. Confidentiality & Non-Solicitation

Manager agrees to keep confidential all Company pricing, compensation structures, store performance data, agent/customer lists, and business methods. Manager agrees not to solicit Company clients, agents, or personnel for any competing business during the term of this Agreement and for twelve (12) months following termination.

6. Termination

This Agreement may be terminated immediately by the Company for: (a) failure to meet the minimum performance standards set forth in Section 3; (b) violation of Company policy or applicable law; (c) breach of confidentiality; or (d) at-will termination as described in Section 4. Upon termination, all compensation earned and vested through the termination date, less any pending chargeback holds, will be paid per the standard payment schedule.

7. Acknowledgment

By signing below, Manager acknowledges that they have read, understand, and agree to the compensation structure, the performance minimums, and the termination provisions described in this Agreement, and understand that this is a performance-based, commission-driven independent contractor role with no flat salary or guaranteed weekly bonus.
